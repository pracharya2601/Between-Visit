# Phase 19 Requirements: Distinct-Landmark Warning + Demo Landmark Fix
Version: phase-19 (starts after Phase 18 is reported)

Goal: practitioners are warned when two checkpoints of a route read alike (which confuses clients and picture matching), and can ask Sonnet for a more distinctive description. The synthetic demo route gets three distinct landmark texts. Phases 1–18 must keep working.

## Warning
- REQ-591 [always] The route builder, teach review and scan placement shall check every pair of checkpoints in the same route.
- REQ-592 [always] A pair is "too alike" when the content-word overlap of their landmark texts is high (Jaccard ≥ 0.6 after lower-casing and stopword removal, interpreted) OR their photos are very similar (perceptual hash Hamming distance ≤ 10 of 64, interpreted).
- REQ-593 [state] While a pair is too alike, both cards shall show: "Reads like checkpoint N. Add what is different here (a sign, a door, a turn, a sound)." (N = the other checkpoint's order; if several, list them, e.g. "checkpoints 2 and 3", interpreted).
- REQ-594 [always] The warning shall update when texts or photos change, and shall be announced politely to screen readers (not assertive).
- REQ-595 [always] Saving with a warning shall still be allowed; the warning never blocks Approve or Save.

## Suggest a different description
- REQ-596 [always] A card with the warning shall offer "Suggest a different description".
- REQ-597 [event] When pressed, the system shall send Sonnet (the same model/config as landmark suggestions) this checkpoint's photo plus the OTHER checkpoints' landmark texts, asking for one stable description (≤25 words) that names what is unique here compared with the others; the person rule applies ("Retake: a person is in the photo").
- REQ-598 [event] When a suggestion returns, the system shall put it in the text as a "Suggested by AI" proposal; since the text changed, the checkpoint becomes unapproved (Phase 3 rule) and the practitioner must approve again.
- REQ-599 [unwanted] If the AI fails, then the system shall show "AI suggestion unavailable" and keep the current text.

## Demo route fix (one-off, synthetic demo data only, both v1 and v2)
- REQ-600 [event] When the migration runs, the system shall give each checkpoint of "Entrance → hallway turn → reception desk (synthetic)" its own text from its own frame: Entrance → the push-bar entrance door and the start of the handrail; Hallway turn → the corner where the corridor turns right (and the RECEPTION sign if visible); Reception desk → the desk at the end.
- REQ-601 [always] The source frames shall be the matching clean synthetic recording frames (e.g. frame 1, the turn frame, the last frame), which also become the checkpoint photos for those checkpoints. (interpreted)
- REQ-602 [always] The texts shall be generated by Sonnet with the distinctiveness prompt, then fixed in the data (≤25 words each), applied to the checkpoints collection and the v1 and v2 snapshots; idempotent, logged.
- REQ-603 [always] Past logged events stay as they were (they record what was said at the time). (interpreted)
- REQ-604 [always] After the fix: no "Reads like…" warning on the demo route; v1 speaks the chair 2/2; v2 holds it back 2/2; the checkpoints progress Entrance → Hallway turn → Reception desk.

## Distance guard (user-approved after the Phase 19 finding)
- REQ-605 [unwanted] If the route version has a position for the matched checkpoint, the pose is fresh (<1 s) and aligned, and the client is more than 3.0 m (x/z) from that checkpoint, then the system shall ignore that picture match for progress (log a silent cue "picture_too_far", routine, hidden). It applies to next and next+1 (skip) matches.
- REQ-606 [state] While the version has no checkpoint positions, the pose is stale or missing, or a scan-route run isn't aligned yet (<1.5 m walked), the guard shall be off and picture matches count as normal.
- REQ-607 [always] The guard constant shall be a single setting (PICTURE_MAX_DIST_M = 3.0).
- REQ-608 [always] After the guard: demo v1 speaks the chair 2/2, v2 holds it back 2/2, the checkpoints progress in order; My room is unaffected.
